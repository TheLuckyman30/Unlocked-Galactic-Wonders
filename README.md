# Unlocked Galactic Wonders

This is a simple Stellaris mod that allows you to research and construct a dyson sphere, ringworld, and matter decompressor without having to take the galactic wonders ascension perk. This does render the galactic wonders ascension perk completely useless.

- The month after you complete your first megastructure (from scratch or rebuilt), you will receive a prompt to select between the dyson sphere, ringworld, and matter decompressor
- Upon selection, you will receive a special project to research the megastructure you selected in the previous step. 
- After completing the special project, you will be able to build the megastructure you selected, and you will gain the other two as permanent research options
